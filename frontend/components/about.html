About BigBully AI

Democratizing stock research for every Indian investor

Our Story

BigBully AI was founded with a simple mission: to make stock research accessible and understandable for every Indian investor.

We realized that most retail investors struggle with information overload and complex financial jargon. BigBully AI uses advanced AI to simplify financial information and help investors understand any company in just 2 minutes.

Today, we serve over 500K Indian investors across NSE and BSE, providing AI-powered insights that actually make sense.

🎯

Our Mission

Build India's fastest AI-powered stock research and decision support platform that saves research time, reduces information overload, and builds user trust through transparent, ethical AI.

🚀

Our Vision

Every Indian investor understands the stocks they invest in. We envision a future where complex financial information is simplified, accessible, and actionable for everyone.
